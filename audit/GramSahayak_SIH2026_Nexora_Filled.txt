ITERATED SOLUTION SMART INDIA HACKATHON 2026  |  KUMARAGURU INTERNAL IDEATHON DOUBLE DIAMOND SUBMISSION TEMPLATE Solution overview GramSahayak  AI:  A two-tier decision platform that helps rural entrepreneurs evaluate business feasibility and structure financing  before  investing, using verified local data and 100% accurate financial rules. Solution architecture / workflow 1️⃣ User Input (The Starting Point) What it is:  A simple 3-step form. How it works:  The rural entrepreneur just enters three things:  Location  (Village),  Capital  (e.g., ₹50,000), and  Business Idea  (e.g., Dairy). Why it matters:  Keeps the UI extremely simple for users with low digital literacy. 2️⃣ Local Intelligence Engine (The Data Gatherer) What it is:  The system's connection to real-world data. How it works:  It automatically pulls  Census data  (to estimate local population/households) and queries  OpenStreetMap (OSM)  (to count existing similar businesses within a 5km radius). Why it matters:  This ensures our AI is grounded in  real data , not making up fake village statistics (no hallucinations). 3️⃣ Feasibility Engine (The "Business Brain") What it is:  The analytical core that checks if the business makes sense. How it works:  It uses the data from Step 2 to calculate a  Purchasing Power Index  (proxy math). Then, it uses an  LLM (AI)  to generate a customized SWOT analysis and risk report based  only  on those verified numbers. Why it matters:  Tells the user if there is actual market demand and who their local competitors are. 4️⃣ Financial Engine (The "Strict Calculator") What it is:  A 100% deterministic math module.  Zero AI is used here. How it works:  It takes the user's capital, calculates the total project cost (Capital ÷ 10%), determines the loan amount (90%), and applies the exact official interest rates (6.5% or 8%) and moratorium periods to calculate the exact quarterly EMI. Why it matters:  Financial advice must be 100% accurate. By keeping AI out of the math, we eliminate financial hallucinations.
